V-M2. A validated colour for a third sport

The Dashboard's weekly load chart at 375 px, with Strength added (see issue #46 and "Other training"). Fixed order: running, climbing, strength. Switch your OS to dark for the dark steps.

Trade-off: no hue clears both the sport checks and a ΔE of 15 from every gym grade, because grades already use yellow, green, blue, purple and red. Indigo is ΔE 6.9 from grade blue. That is acceptable because sport colours and grade colours never share a chart, a sport is always shown with its name and icon, and a grade always with its name and ringed dot. If that ever changes, Strength should switch to the 45° texture fill instead of a new hue.
