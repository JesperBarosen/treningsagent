V-M3. Summary first, details one tap away

Dashboard at 375 px, first screen. High visual complexity lowered first-impression appeal the most (Reinecke et al. 2013), and older users preferred low complexity. Uses the sport filter from issue #46.

Change: on mobile, the Dashboard opens with the filter, three headline numbers and one sentence built from the backend's numbers, and each chart becomes a row that expands in place with its trend chip visible. From 768 px the current grid stays, since there is room. Colour use does not change; the reduction is in the number of things on the first screen. See design-visual.md, V-M3.
