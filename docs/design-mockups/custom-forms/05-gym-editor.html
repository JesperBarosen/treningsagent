Gym editor: ordered colours with approximate ranges

Editing Klatreverket at 375 px: colours easiest first, each with a name, a palette swatch and an approximate V or Font range. Variant A and B are alternative G2 in custom-log-forms.md, section 6.5.

Variant ARange as two selects

Recommended. Compact and works with screen readers out of the box.

Variant BRange on a V ruler

Tap the lowest and highest grade; overlaps between colours are visible at a glance.

Colours come from the screen-safe grade palette; orange and pink are candidates that need the colour-blind checks (#55) first. A used colour is archived, never deleted, so old climbs keep their name. The range is stored as difficulty-index values, so switching to Font only changes the labels. See custom-log-forms.md, section 3.4.
