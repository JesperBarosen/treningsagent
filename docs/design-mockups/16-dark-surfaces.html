V-M1. Dark surfaces: a little lighter, and a lighter sheet on top

Dashboard with a session sheet open, dark theme, 375 px. Apple's dark mode uses dimmer "base" and brighter "elevated" backgrounds so a layer on top reads as nearer; Material 3 does the same with tonal elevation.

Change: lift the dark canvas and surface one small step, add an --elevated token for sheets, dialogs and the fixed composer, and soften ink slightly. The light theme does not change. Light stays the better default for reading where the OS asks for it (Piepenbrock et al. 2013; Gazit et al. 2025); this only makes the dark theme less harsh for the people who choose it.
