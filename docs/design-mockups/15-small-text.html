V-S2 and V-S4. Small secondary text: 7:1 and 13 px

Sessions at 375 px. Switch your OS to dark to compare the dark values. The positive-polarity advantage is largest for small characters (Piepenbrock et al. 2014), and Apple asks custom colours to "strive for a contrast ratio of 7:1, especially in small text".

Change: darken --ink-muted in light and lighten it in dark so secondary text reaches 7:1 on every background it sits on, and add a 13 px "meta" step for captions that carry information (dates, weekly totals, trend lines). Chart ticks and purely decorative labels can stay at 12 px. See design-visual.md, V-S2 and V-S4.
